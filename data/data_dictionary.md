# Data dictionary
- `loan_id`: unique loan identifier.
- `reporting_month`: calendar month the row describes.
- `origination_month`: month the loan was originated.
- `loan_age_months`: months since origination.
- `remaining_term_months`: contractual months remaining.
- `original_balance`: principal at origination.
- `current_balance`: unpaid principal at the reporting month.
- `interest_rate`: note rate in percent.
- `credit_score`: borrower credit score at observation.
- `credit_score_band`: discretised credit score.
- `ltv_band`: loan-to-value band at origination.
- `dti_band`: debt-to-income band at origination.
- `state`: property state.
- `loan_purpose`: purchase / refinance / cash-out.
- `occupancy_type`: owner / investor / second home.
- `property_type`: single-family / condo / townhouse.
- `servicer_name`: servicing entity.
- `current_status`: performing status or delinquency stage.
- `days_past_due`: days past due at the reporting month.
- `document_status`: completeness of loan documents.
- `last_updated_at`: timestamp of the last system update.
- `source_system`: originating system of the row.
